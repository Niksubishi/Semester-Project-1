## About the Project
Welcome to **Community Science Museum**.
<br><br>
 The Community Science Museum website was crafted to specifically cater to its audience of children aged 7-15 and families with younger kids. It offers a sleek, accessible, and fully responsive design, providing essential details about the museum, its exhibits, and opportunities for visitor participation. The site’s primary goal is to spark interest and encourage people to visit by offering visually engaging and informative content.

### Main Features     
* **Interactive Homepage**:  
 The homepage showcases vibrant visuals and highlights the museum's mission, along with upcoming events, designed to engage both children and families from the moment they arrive.

* **Exhibitions & Involvement**:  
Visitors can explore information about future exhibitions and discover ways to support the museum, whether through donations or active participation in its initiatives.  

* **Get in Touch**:  
The website features a dedicated contact section with essential information such as the museum’s location, phone number, operating hours, and a user-friendly form for inquiries.
  
* **Privacy Policy & Terms and Conditions**:  
A section is devoted to outlining the museum’s privacy practices and terms of service, ensuring transparency for all visitors.
